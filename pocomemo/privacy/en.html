Privacy Policy

日本語 | English

Pocosoft Inc. (株式会社ポコソフト; "we", "us" or "our") sets out in this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") how we handle users' personal information in the services provided through the PocoMemo app (the "Service").

This English version is a translation of the Japanese version. If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

Article 1 (Personal Information)

"Personal information" means personal information as defined in Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information: information about a living individual that can identify a specific person through a name, date of birth, address, telephone number, contact details or other description, and information that can identify a specific person on its own, such as data on appearance, fingerprints or voiceprints, or a health insurance number.

Article 2 (How We Collect Personal Information)

We may ask for personal information such as your name, date of birth, address, telephone number and email address when you register or when we investigate a problem. We may also collect transaction records and payment information containing your personal information from our partners, including information providers, advertisers and ad delivery partners ("Partners").

Article 3 (Purposes of Collection and Use)

We collect and use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate our services
  • To answer your inquiries, including verifying your identity
  • To send emails about new features, updates and campaigns for the services you use, and about our other services
  • To contact you when necessary, for example about maintenance or important notices
  • To identify users who violate our terms of use or try to use the Service for fraudulent or improper purposes, and to refuse their use
  • To let you view, change or delete your registered information and view your usage status
  • To charge fees for paid services
  • For purposes incidental to the above

Article 4 (Changes to Purposes of Use)

We change the purposes of use of personal information only where the new purposes can reasonably be considered related to the previous ones. If we change them, we will notify you of the new purposes in a manner we determine.

Article 5 (Provision to Third Parties)

Except in the following cases, or as permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other laws, we do not provide personal information to third parties without your prior consent:

  • Where it is necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  • Where it is especially necessary to improve public health or promote the healthy development of children and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  • Where it is necessary to cooperate with a national or local government body, or a party entrusted by one, in carrying out duties prescribed by law, and obtaining your consent could interfere with those duties
  • Where we have announced or published the following in advance and notified the Personal Information Protection Commission: that the purposes of use include provision to third parties; the items of data provided; the means or method of provision; that provision will stop at the request of the individual; and how such requests are received

Despite the above, the recipient is not treated as a third party in the following cases:

  • Where we entrust all or part of the handling of personal information within the scope needed to achieve the purposes of use
  • Where personal information is provided as part of a business succession resulting from a merger or other cause
  • Where personal information is used jointly with specific parties and we have notified you, or made easily available to you, that fact, the items used, the scope of joint users, their purposes of use, and the name of the party responsible for managing the information

Article 6 (Disclosure)

If you ask us to disclose your personal information, we will do so without delay. We may withhold all or part of it, and will notify you without delay, if disclosure would fall under any of the following. Subject to Article 11, we charge a fee of 10,000 yen per request for disclosure.

  • It could harm the life, body, property or other rights and interests of you or a third party
  • It could seriously interfere with the proper conduct of our business
  • It would otherwise violate laws or regulations

Despite the above, we generally do not disclose information other than personal information, such as history and attribute information.

Article 7 (Correction and Deletion)

If the personal information we hold about you is wrong, you may ask us to correct, add to or delete it ("Correction") under the procedure we set. If we decide a request should be met, we will make the Correction without delay. We will notify you without delay when we have made a Correction or decided not to.

Article 8 (Suspension of Use)

If you ask us to stop using or to erase your personal information ("Suspension") because it is being handled beyond the purposes of use or was obtained improperly, we will investigate without delay. If the results show that the request should be met, we will carry out the Suspension without delay, and will notify you without delay when we have done so or decided not to. Where Suspension would be very costly or otherwise difficult, and an alternative measure can protect your rights and interests, we will take that alternative measure.

Article 9 (Error Reports from the Windows Version)

To investigate problems and improve quality, the Windows version of the app sends the following information to us through Sentry, a service provided by Functional Software, Inc.:

  • When the app quits unexpectedly or an error occurs in the app, the details of the error and the actions just before it (such as opening a screen or signing in)
  • App launches and exits (to count users and the share of sessions that ended without a crash)
  • The app version, the OS version and an anonymous identifier created for each device

The information sent does not include the contents of your memos, your name, email address or Apple ID, or the name of your computer. If error details contain the name of one of your folders, it is masked before sending. We do not record the IP address the information is sent from. This information cannot identify a specific individual, and we use it only to investigate problems and improve quality. The information sent is stored on Sentry's servers in the United States.

Article 10 (Advertising and Analytics)

The Service uses the following external services. Through them, the information below is sent from your device to Google and handled under Google's policies. We do not use this information to identify specific individuals by matching it with names, email addresses or similar details.

  • Google AdMob (advertising in the iOS version): device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking as described below), approximate location estimated from your IP address, interactions such as ad views and taps, advertising data, crash data and performance data. This is used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and analytics.
  • Firebase Analytics (analytics in the iOS version, and in macOS versions up to 2.0.0): app instance ID, approximate location estimated from your IP address, interactions such as app launches and screen views, in-app purchase events, and device and OS information. We use this to understand how the Service is used and to improve it.

The memos you create are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account. We do not collect their contents and do not send them to the services above.

In the iOS version, we ask for your permission to track through App Tracking Transparency. The advertising identifier is used only if you allow it, in which case information about your device may be combined with information from other companies' apps and websites for advertising. You can change this choice in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent to the use of data for advertising when you use the Service. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Ad Privacy Settings" on the settings screen of the iOS version. This item appears only in regions where consent is required.

In iOS versions later than 2.0.0, in regions where consent is required, analytics through Firebase Analytics does not run until you consent to it, and it stops if you withdraw your consent in "Ad Privacy Settings". In iOS version 2.0.0, analytics runs in every region.

macOS versions later than 2.0.0 do not perform analytics. macOS versions up to 2.0.0 perform analytics through Firebase Analytics in every region.

While you are subscribed to PocoMemo Plus, we do not show ads or request them. Analytics continues during a subscription under the conditions above.

This information may be handled on servers outside Japan, including in the United States. For how Google handles information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
